User Manual: Home Screen Refinement (v2.4.10)

Improved Home Screen Greeting

In this update, we have refined your Home Screen layout to make it cleaner and more readable. Your greeting and name are now separated into two clear lines:

  • Line 1: Temporal greeting (e.g., "Good morning").
  • Line 2: Your Full Name in a bold, prominent font.

This layout ensures that your primary identity is highlighted immediately upon opening the app.

Privacy Controls

Your name remains hidden by default for your privacy. To show your name, tap the eye icon located to the right of your name. Your name will be masked again the next time you open the app to ensure your personal information is protected from observers.
