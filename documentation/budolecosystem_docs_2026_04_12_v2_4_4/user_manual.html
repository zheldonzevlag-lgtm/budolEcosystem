User Manual: Understanding Your Wallet Limits

Account Tiers

BudolPay uses tiered accounts to balance security and convenience. Your tier is determined by your verification status.

BASIC (Non-Verified)

Perfect for trying out BudolPay. Limits are as follows:

  • Max Balance: ₱10,000 (Keep your wallet under this amount).
  • Sending Money (P2P): Allowed up to ₱5,000 per month.
  • Adding Money (Cash-In): Allowed up to ₱5,000 per month.
  • Withdrawing (Cash-Out): Not available. Please verify your account.

FULLY VERIFIED

Unlock the full power of BudolPay:

  • Max Balance: No limit.
  • Sending Money (P2P): No monthly limit (Daily limit up to ₱1M).
  • Adding Money (Cash-In): No cumulative monthly limit.
  • Withdrawing (Cash-Out): Enabled for bank transfers and partner outlets.

How to Upgrade

  1. Tap the Profile icon in the BudolPay app.
  2. Select Get Verified.
  3. Submit a valid ID and a quick selfie.
  4. Wait for verification confirmation (usually within 24 hours).
