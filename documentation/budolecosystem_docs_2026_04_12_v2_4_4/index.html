BudolPay Release v2.4.4: Tiered Limits Alignment

Purpose

This release aligns the BASIC (Non-Verified) account tier with market standards established by GCash and other leading e-wallets. The goal is to provide a seamless onboarding experience while maintaining strict regulatory compliance through cumulative monthly volume tracking.

Rationale

Market parity requires that "Basic" (Partially Verified/Non-Verified) accounts have sufficient utility to encourage adoption. Increasing the wallet ceiling to PHP 10,000 and enabling PHP 5,000 in monthly P2P movement provides this utility without exceeding the low-risk profiles defined by the BSP for unverified accounts.

Highlights

  • Tier Upgrade: Basic wallet ceiling increased from ₱5,000 to ₱10,000.
  • Feature Enablement: P2P Transfers enabled for Basic users (previously blocked).
  • Volume Guardrails: New PHP 5,000 monthly cumulative limit for both Inbound (Cash-In) and Outbound (P2P).
  • Forensic Tracking: Monthly volume calculations integrated into the transaction service audit trail.

Compliance Statement

All changes remain within the thresholds specified by BSP Circular No. 808 and Circular No. 1160 (Consumer Protection). Outbound limits for unverified accounts are strictly capped at ₱5,000/month to mitigate AML/CFT risks.
