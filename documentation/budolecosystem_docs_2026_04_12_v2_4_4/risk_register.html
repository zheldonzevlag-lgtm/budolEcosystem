Risk Register: Tiered Limits (v2.4.4)

Risk ID Description Impact Probability Mitigation Strategy
R-2.4.4-01 Smurfing via multiple BASIC accounts MEDIUM LOW Phonenumber uniqueness and AI Behavioral Scoring (calculateRiskScore) detection.
R-2.4.4-02 Cumulative volume tracking failure HIGH LOW Unit tested getMonthlyCumulativeVolume with Prisma aggregation.
R-2.4.4-03 Unauthorized P2P movement by Non-Verified users MEDIUM MEDIUM Strict ₱5,000 monthly cap enforced at API level before fund movement.
R-2.4.4-04 Wallet balance ceiling bypass MEDIUM LOW Atomic checks in /cash-in and /transfer prevent balance overflow for BASIC users.

Note on Compliance: These risks are handled in accordance with BSP Circular 706 regarding risk-based KYC procedures.
