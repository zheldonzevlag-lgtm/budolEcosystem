Institutional Roadmap (v2.4.1+)

Strategic Evolution of the Behavioral Anomaly Engine

High Priority

Peer-Group Clustering

Instead of global medians, implement cohort-based baselining (e.g., student accounts vs. merchant accounts). This significantly reduces false positives for high-velocity institutional users.

High Priority

Automated Escalation Logic

Integrate with the Notification Service to trigger SMS/Email alerts to users when a transaction is placed in FLAGGED_REVIEW, providing transparency and reducing support ticket volume.

Medium Priority

Institutional Trust Scoring

Weight positive history (verified income, consistent behavior) to reduce the score contribution of minor spikes, allowing longer-tenured users more operational freedom.

Medium Priority

Predictive Forensics

Utilize the riskMetadata aggregate to predict potential account takeovers before the final fund-out step (Phase 5 goal).
