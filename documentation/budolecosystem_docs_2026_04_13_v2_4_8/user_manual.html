Mobile App User Guide (v2.4.8)

Version v2.4.8 improves the stability of your daily transactions.

💰 Stable Cash-In

You can now safely check your Allowed Cash In limit on the Cash In screen. The application will no longer crash when viewing these limits, regardless of your current balance.

📊 Real-time Limit Tracking

For BASIC Tier accounts, the monthly sending limit of ₱5,000 and wallet limit of ₱10,000 are accurately calculated and displayed in real-time on the Send Money and Cash In screens.

🧩 Admin Login

The Admin Dashboard login challenge is now more responsive. If you enter a wrong answer, the challenge will automatically refresh with new numbers after 1.5 seconds.
