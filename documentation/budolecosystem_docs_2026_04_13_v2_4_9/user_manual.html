User Manual: Full Name Display (v2.4.9)

1. New Home Screen Greeting

Your greeting now displays your full legal name (First Name and Last Name) instead of just your first name or email address. This provides a more personal and professional environment for your financial transactions.

2. Privacy Masking

By default, your name is hidden behind a privacy mask (••••••). Tap the Eye Icon next to your name to show or hide it. Your name is always masked on app launch for maximum privacy.

3. Setting your Legal Name

If you signed up with an email and haven't set your name yet, follow these steps:

  1. Go to Settings (gear icon).
  2. Tap on Edit Profile.
  3. Fill in your First and Last names.
  4. Tap Save Changes.

Once saved, your Home Screen will instantly update to show your Full Name.
