Future Enhancements & Recommendations (v2.4.6)

Identified Technical Debt & Next Steps

  • Backend E2E Tests: Several backend Jest tests (e.g., `realtime.test.js`, `verify_admin_role.test.ts`, `spam_prevention.test.js`) are failing due to missing credentials, parser issues and environment constraints. These need to be triaged and fixed to ensure high CI/CD reliability.
  • Admin Dashboard: Continue refining the compliance and risk management modules, including pagination for lists and implementing bulk actions for transaction approvals.
