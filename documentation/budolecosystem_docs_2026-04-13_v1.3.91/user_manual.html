User Manual: The 'Modern Ruby' Experience

Welcome to the premium BudolPay experience. Version 1.3.91 restores our classic Ruby identity with next-generation visual depth.

Premium Mesh Backgrounds

Enjoy a visually stunning login and registration experience with dynamic mesh gradients. These animations are designed to be high-performance while providing a premium, secure atmosphere.

Frosted Glass Balance Card

Your main dashboard now features a sophisticated glassmorphism balance card. The frosted-glass effect provides a clear view of your available funds with institutional-grade legibility.

Enhanced Focus States

We've improved visibility for all input fields. When you tap to type, the field will glow with a prominent Ruby border, ensuring you always know exactly where you are in the process.

How to Verify Your App

1. Open the app and check the splash screen.
2. Look for the version number v1.3.91 at the bottom of the Settings screen or on the login logo.
3. Confirm that the primary accent color is Budol Red (#F43F5E).
