What changed for you as a buyer on budolShap?

When you cancel a QR payment — by clicking the X button, tapping Cancel Payment, or letting the 10-minute countdown expire — budolShap now correctly records the payment as Cancelled in all connected systems. Previously, the payment could appear as Pending even after you cancelled.

How to Cancel a QR Payment

1
Proceed to checkout and select budolPay as your payment method.
2
A QR code will appear on screen. You have 10 minutes to scan it using the budolPay mobile app.
3
If you decide not to pay, tap the × (Close) button or the Cancel Payment link.
4
budolShap will cancel both your order and the payment record.
5
You will see a confirmation toast: "Payment cancelled. Your cart items are still saved."
6
Your cart items remain — you can choose a different payment method or continue shopping.

What if the timer expires?

If you do not scan the QR code within 10 minutes, the session expires automatically. The payment is cancelled and you are redirected back to your cart. No charge is made.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I be charged?

No. A QR payment only processes when you scan and confirm it inside the budolPay app. Closing the modal or letting it expire makes no charge.

Can I retry the payment after cancelling?

Yes. Return to your cart, review your order, and proceed to checkout again to generate a new QR code.

The Admin Dashboard still shows PENDING — what should I do?

With v2.2.8 deployed, this should no longer occur. If you see a PENDING transaction for a cancelled order, please contact your system administrator and reference issue #001 in the Issues & Fixes log.
