Ensuring Your Security

Learn how BudolPay protects your funds with AI-Driven Behavioral Monitoring.

Why was my transaction flagged?

BudolPay uses advanced AI to learn your typical spending habits. If a transaction occurs that is significantly different from your usual behavior (for example, a very large transfer compared to your normal activity), we may place a temporary hold on the funds to ensure it was truly authorized by you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a security hold last?

Most holds are reviewed by our professional compliance team within 30 to 60 minutes during standard institutional hours.

Do I need to do anything?

In most cases, no. Our team will verify the transaction against your security profile. If we need further verification, we will contact you via your registered email or phone number.

Is my money safe?

Absolutely. Transaction holds are designed specifically to prevent unauthorized movement of your funds in the event of an account compromise.
