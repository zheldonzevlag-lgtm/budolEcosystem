Institutional Anomaly Management v2.4.0

This document guides compliance officers through the resolution of AI-flagged behavioral anomalies.

CRITICAL: Transactions with a Risk Score of 90+ are automatically held and funds are NOT settled until institutional approval is granted.

Manual Resolution Workflow

1
Monitor the Compliance Board

Navigate to the Compliance Board on the Admin Dashboard. New anomalies will appear in real-time with a "Critical Anomaly" badge.

2
Verify Forensic Metadata

Hover over the risk gauge to see the Deviation % and Method. This explains why the AI flagged the user (e.g., "10x Baseline Spike").

3
Institutional Action

Click APPROVE to clear the hold and settle funds, or REJECT to bounce the transaction and notify the user of a security hold.

Administrative Configuration

  • Blocking Threshold: Global setting is Score >= 90.
  • Flagging Threshold: Global setting is Score >= 75 (Visual alert only).
