Strategic Roadmap & Recommendations v2.4.0

Future iterations for the BudolPay AI Behavioral Framework.

1. Multi-Dimensional Risk Scoring MEDIUM TERM

Transition from univariate analysis (Amount only) to multi-variate analysis including Geographic Velocity (GPS movement speed between logins) and Device Fingerprinting anomalies.

2. Peer-Group Clustering LONG TERM

Integrate K-Means Clustering to group users by economic profile (e.g., Micro-Entrepreneurs vs. Corporate Remitters). Scoring will then compare behavior against peer-group medians rather than a global system median.

3. Automated Escalation (Push Notifications) IMMEDIATE

Enable real-time push-notification alerts for users when a transaction is held, allowing them to verify identity via Biometrics (FaceID/TouchID) to instantly lift AI-imposed holds.

4. Explainable AI (XAI) UI NEXT ITERATION

Enhance the ComplianceBoard to visualize the EWMA curve in a chart format, helping compliance officers visually understand why a specific point was considered an outlier.
