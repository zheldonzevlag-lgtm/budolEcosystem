Institutional Compliance Audit: v2.4.3

Standard: BSP Circular 808/1213 Audit Date: 2026-04-11

1. Separation of Duties (Maker-Checker)

Requirement: Critical financial operations must require the involvement of at least two authorized individuals.

Implementation: The /resolve endpoint enforces a state-machine where a transaction in FLAGGED_REVIEW cannot be finalized until it has both a Proposer (MANAGER) and an Authorizer (GENERAL_MANAGER). Identification is verified via session-backed role checks.

2. Forensic Audit Traceability

Requirement: All manual overrides of automated risk flags must be fully documented and attributable.

Implementation: Audit logs capture the IDs of both the Maker and the Checker, the Forensic Reason Code, and the precise timestamp of both steps. These records are stored in the AuditLog table with a system_resolution category.

3. AML/TF Risk Mitigation

Requirement: Manual resolutions must not bypass systemic AML controls.

Implementation: Only transactions explicitly moved to FLAGGED_REVIEW (due to HVT or Anomaly triggers) are eligible for resolution. Completion logic ensures that the transaction state is immutable once finalized, preventing double-resolution attacks.
