System Admin Manual: Institutional Resolution

Version: 2.4.3 Last Updated: 2026-04-11 Author: Antigravity AI

1. Role Overview

The manual transaction resolution workflow is divided into two distinct institutional roles to satisfy BSP Circular 1213 (Separation of Duties).

Role Function Responsibility
MANAGER Maker (Proposer) Reviews flagging reasons, attaches investigation notes, and proposes an APPROVE or REJECT action.
GENERAL_MANAGER Checker (Authorizer) Performs a secondary review of the proposal and notes. Executes the final authorization to disperse or block funds.

2. Operational Workflow

Step 1: Alert Review (Maker)

  1. Navigate to the Compliance Board in the Admin Dashboard.
  2. Identify transactions with FLAGGED_REVIEW status.
  3. Review the Risk Score and Anomaly Reasons (e.g., HVT, Velocity, Geographic Leap).
  4. Click Propose Resolution.
  5. Select action and provide a detailed Forensic Reason Code.

Step 2: Dual-Party Authorization (Checker)

  1. A General Manager logs into the dashboard.
  2. Identifies transactions marked with a Proposed Action in the metadata.
  3. Verifies that the Proposer is NOT themselves (unilateral overrides are blocked by the system).
  4. Clicks Authorize Resolution to finalize the fund movement.

3. Approved Reason Codes

Admins MUST use one of the following codes when proposing/authorizing:

  • KYC_VERIFIED_HVT: High-Value Transfer verified through secondary KYC call.
  • LEGITIMATE_MERCHANT: Account verified as a known high-volume merchant.
  • AML_MATCH_CONFIRMED: Transaction rejected due to AML/Watchlist hit.
  • DATA_ENTRY_CORRECTION: Manual adjustment for systemic rounding or display errors.
  • FRAUD_SUSPICION_CONFIRMED: Account flagged for suspicious activity patterns.

4. Security & Compliance

BSP Circular 808 Any resolution performed without the two-step authorization is considered a violation of the Institutional Risk Profile.

Audit Logs: Every step is logged in the AuditLog table with IP attribution and role-based metadata. These logs are immutable and ready for BIR/BSP inspection.
