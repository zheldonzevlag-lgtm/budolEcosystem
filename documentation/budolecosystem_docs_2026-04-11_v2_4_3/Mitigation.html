Institutional Mitigations: Financial Integrity

Phase: 6 (Audit) Classification: Internal Only

1. Forensic Reason Enforcement

Threat: Admins overriding alerts with generic or misleading reasons (e.g., "OK").

Mitigation: The /resolve endpoint now mandates a minimum of 10 characters for the reason field and encourages the use of established Institutional Reason Codes. This ensures that a human-readable forensic trail exists for every override.

2. Anti-Collusion ID Comparison

Threat: A single user trying to both PROPOSE and AUTHORIZE a resolution.

Mitigation: Hard-coded identity check in the transaction-service rejects any authorization attempt where the adminId matches the proposedBy ID stored in the risk metadata.

if (adminId === tx.riskMetadata.proposedBy) throw Error("Unilateral authorization blocked.");

3. Multi-Tiered Transaction Validation

Threat: Malicious resolution of an already failing transaction.

Mitigation: The resolution logic verifies that the transaction is in FLAGGED_REVIEW before allowing any proposal or authorization. This prevents state-injection attacks on PENDING or COMPLETED transactions.
