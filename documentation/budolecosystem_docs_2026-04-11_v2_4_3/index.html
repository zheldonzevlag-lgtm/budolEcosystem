Institutional Resolution Protocol v2.4.3

Phase: 6 (Documentation) Release Date: 2026-04-11 Status: STABLE

Executive Summary

Version 2.4.3 operationalizes the "Four-Eyes Principle" (Maker-Checker) for manual transaction resolution. This institutional upgrade ensures that high-risk anomalies are proposed by a Manager and authorized by a General Manager, preventing unilateral resolution of suspicious transactions and aligning BudolPay with BSP and international banking standards.

Core Features

  • Institutional RBAC: Introduction of MANAGER and GENERAL_MANAGER roles.
  • Separation of Duties: Mandates a two-step resolution workflow for transactions in FLAGGED_REVIEW.
  • Forensic Traceability: Multi-party attribution in audit logs for all manual overrides.
  • Balance Safety: Funds are only dispersed AFTER final institutional clearance.

Regulatory Alignment

BSP Circular 808 BSP Circular 1213 AMLA Compliance PCI-DSS 10.2.x

The institutional resolution protocol satisfies the requirement for stringent internal controls over financial dispersals and ensures that no single individual has complete control over a sensitive transaction lifecycle.
