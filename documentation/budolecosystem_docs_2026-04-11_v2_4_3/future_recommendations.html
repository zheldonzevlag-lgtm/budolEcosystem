Roadmap: Institutional Security Hardening

Phase: Post-v2.4.3 Priority: Strategic

1. Biometric-Backed Authorization (BBA)

Proposal: Integrate hardware-level biometric validation (FIDO2 / WebAuthn) for high-value transaction authorizations by GENERAL_MANAGER roles.

Rationale: Prevents session hijacking or credential theft from bypassing the final checker step. Ensures the authorizer is physically present. Fits under Phase 3 of the CyberSecurity Law compliance roadmap.

2. ML-Based Resolution Prediction

Proposal: Utilize historical resolution data to provide "Confidence Scores" to Managers during the Maker step.

Rationale: Assists Managers in identifying legitimate high-value users based on 12-month behavioral context, reducing operational friction for VIP clients.

3. Time-Locked Resolution Corridors

Proposal: Restrict manual resolutions to institutional banking hours (9 AM - 5 PM) for non-emergency HVT overrides.

Rationale: Prevents "Midnight Siphoning" where transactions are resolved during low-oversight periods. Aligns with BSP intraday liquidity reporting standards.
