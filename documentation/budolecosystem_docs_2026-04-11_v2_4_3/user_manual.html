Compliance Transparency: What "Held for Review" Means

Version: 2.4.3 Type: End-User Guidance

Why is my transaction held?

BudolPay utilizes institutional AI to monitor for high-risk activity in real-time. A transaction may be held if it triggers compliance thresholds such as:

  • High-Value Transfer (HVT): Single transfers exceeding standard institutional limits.
  • Velocity Anomaly: Rapid succession of transfers beyond normal behavior.
  • Security Hold: Access from a new, untrusted device or geographic Leap.

The Resolution Process

Once a transaction is HELD FOR REVIEW, it undergoes our institutional **Four-Eyes** verification:

  1. Maker Review: A transaction manager evaluates the transfer's legitimacy.
  2. Checker Authorization: A senior executive performs the final clearance.

This process typically completes within 10-60 minutes during institutional hours.

Your Protection

These holds protect your funds from unauthorized access. No funds are permanently dispersed until the dual-party institutional authorization is complete. This is in compliance with BSP Consumer Protection Standards.
