Institutional Risk Register: Maker-Checker Workflow

Version: 2.4.3 Last Updated: 2026-04-11 Classification: Restricted

This register identifies risks associated with the manual transaction resolution process and the institutional RBAC roles introduced in v2.4.3.

Risk ID: RBAC-01 - Internal Collusion

Description: A Manager and a General Manager collude to authorize a fraudulent high-value transaction.

Impact: HIGH (Financial Loss, Regulatory Sanction)

Likelihood: LOW (Requires dual-compromise or collusion)

Mitigation:

  • Mandatory Forensic Reason Codes for every override.
  • Automated audit logging with IP and User Agent tracking.
  • Periodic independent audit of "Resolution Corridors" (frequent resolution pairs).

Risk ID: RBAC-02 - Stale Decisions

Description: A resolution is proposed by a Manager but remains pending in the queue for too long, causing customer friction or bypassing current risk state.

Impact: MEDIUM (Customer Dissatisfaction, Operational Inefficiency)

Mitigation:

  • Proposals older than 24 hours are auto-rejected by the cleanup-stale-proposals worker.
  • Real-time notifications sent to General Managers on Discord/Slack channel.

Risk ID: RBAC-03 - Unilateral Role Escalation

Description: A technical staff member manually updates their role to GENERAL_MANAGER to bypass Maker-Checker.

Impact: CRITICAL (System Compromise)

Mitigation:

  • Role updates are blocked at the SSO level (BudolID) and require Admin-tier authorization.
  • Audit logs for any change to the role field in the User table.
