What's New in v2.5.0

1. Personalized Greeting

You can now choose how your app greets you. Toggle between a bold 'Vertical' layout or a sleek 'Classic' horizontal layout.

To change: Go to Settings > Appearance > Greeting Layout.

2. Profile Progress Tracker

Unlock the full power of BudolPay! Our new progress widget on the home screen shows you exactly what's left to reach 100% verification.

Tip: Completing your KYC (Identity Verification) is the fastest way to get to 100%.

3. Adaptive Dark Mode

Your eyes will thank you. The entire app—including Wallet and Transfer screens—now perfectly adapts to your device's dark mode settings.
