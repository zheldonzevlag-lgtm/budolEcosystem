Forensic Accountability & Audit Hardening

This release addresses the critical requirement for individual accountability in administrative and security-level operations. By enforcing actor attribution across the Budol Ecosystem, we ensure compliance with BSP Circular 808 and PCI DSS 10.2.

Core Objectives

  • Complete forensic attribution for account provisioning.
  • Auditor identity tracing for regulatory reports (BSP/PCI).
  • Denormalized actor metadata for KYC and profile updates.
  • Unified session-to-audit bridge in Admin UI.
